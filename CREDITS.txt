AI

I used a variety of AI tools over the course of the project. Initially, I used
ChatGPT, Copilot, and Claude Sonnet as far as free credits allowed. The bulk of
the project, especially the netcode, was
written under the tutilege of Gemini Pro. It was a great help with discussing
the theory, debating my options, and troubleshooting my implementation.
Towards the end, I tried a range of agentic tools: Codex and Windsurf/Cascade,
and especially Cursor.
